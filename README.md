  <h1>School Bell Rush — Privacy Policy</h1>
  <p><strong>Package:</strong> com.geekcitizenx.schoolbellrush &bull; <strong>Effective Date:</strong> October 31, 2026</p>

  <h2>1. Overview &amp; Data Minimization</h2>
  <p>School Bell Rush is an arcade runner game built with strict privacy-by-design principles. You can play offline or as a local guest without creating an account. We only collect the minimum data required to save your game progress and display optional leaderboard rankings.</p>

  <h2>2. Information We Collect &amp; How It Is Used</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Gameplay Progress:</strong> Level progress, points (PTS), unlocked skins/accessories, and achievements are stored locally on your device and, if you choose to sign in with Google, synced to Google Cloud Firestore.</li>
    <li><strong>Global Leaderboard (Optional):</strong> Only your chosen Gamer Tag (never your raw email address) and your PT Score are shown on the Global Online Leaderboard.</li>
    <li><strong>Support &amp; Diagnostics (Optional):</strong> If you submit the Support and Feedback form and leave diagnostic info enabled, basic device/app state is included in your email draft so you can review it before sending.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>3. Device Permissions (Least Privilege)</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Internet &amp; Network State:</strong> Used solely to sync optional cloud saves and leaderboard scores.</li>
    <li><strong>Vibrate:</strong> Used for brief haptic feedback during obstacle impacts.</li>
    <li><strong>Post Notifications (Optional):</strong> Requested only if you opt in to receive daily streak or school bell reminders.</li>
    <li><strong>Zero Sensitive Access:</strong> School Bell Rush never requests or accesses your Camera, Microphone, Location, Contacts, SMS, Call Logs, or Photo Library.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>4. No Ads, No Tracking &amp; No Data Selling</h2>
  <p>School Bell Rush contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, does not use cross-app advertising identifiers, and never sells, rents, or shares your personal information with third-party marketers or data brokers.</p>

  <h2>5. User Data Deletion &amp; Account Controls</h2>
  <p>You have full control over your data. You may sign out at any time, delete/reset all local and cloud game data immediately inside the app via <strong>Settings &gt; DELETE / RESET MY GAME DATA</strong>, or request complete account and cloud data deletion by emailing <a href="mailto:jdswafford85@gmail.com">jdswafford85@gmail.com</a>.</p>
